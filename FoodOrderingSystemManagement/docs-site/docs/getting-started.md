# Getting Started

FoodOrder POS is a point-of-sale and back-office system for restaurants — orders, kitchen display, tables, inventory, reporting, and staff settings all in one app.

## Logging in

Open the app in your browser and sign in with the email/password given to you by your organization's admin. What you see after logging in depends on your role:

- **Admin** — full access to every feature listed in this guide.
- **Cashier** — Point of Sale, Orders, Tables.
- **Waiter** — Point of Sale (table service), Orders.
- **Kitchen staff** — Kitchen Display only.
