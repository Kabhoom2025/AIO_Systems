# Reports

Reports give a deeper look at sales performance beyond the Dashboard's daily snapshot.

## What you'll see

- **Hourly breakdown** — order count and revenue for every hour of the day, so you can see your busiest windows.
- **Peak hours** — the top hours ranked by revenue or order volume.
- **Date range filtering** — pull numbers for today, this week, this month, or a custom range.
- **Export** — download report data for use outside the app (e.g. spreadsheets).
