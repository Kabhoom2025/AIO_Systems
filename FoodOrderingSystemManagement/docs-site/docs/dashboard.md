# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the landing screen after login — a quick overview of the restaurant's day plus one-tap shortcuts to the screens you use most.

## What you'll see

- **Quick-action cards** — jump straight to New Order, View Orders, Tables, Waiter, Categories, Food Items, or Settings.
- **Sales snapshot** — today's orders, average order value, and highest sale, with a toggle to switch between daily and monthly views.
- **Order status breakdown** — how many orders are Completed, Pending, or Cancelled right now.
- **Revenue figures** — total revenue and order value range for the selected period.
