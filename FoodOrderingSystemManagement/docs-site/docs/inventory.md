# Inventory

Inventory tracks raw ingredients and supplies — separate from the menu items customers order.

## What you'll see

- **Stock list** with current quantity, unit (kg, liters, pieces, etc.), and a low-stock indicator.
- **Low-stock filter** — quickly see everything that's fallen below its minimum threshold.
- **Adjust stock** — manually correct a quantity, with a note explaining why.
- **Record a purchase** — add newly received stock.
- **Record waste** — deduct spoiled or wasted stock, with a reason.
- **Transfer stock** — move quantity between items or locations.
- **Barcode scanning** — scan an item's barcode to add received stock without manual lookup.
